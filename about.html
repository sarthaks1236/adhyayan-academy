HAMARE BAARE MEIN

Shahpur ka ek addha jahan preparation, discipline se milti hai

Adhyayan Academy, Shahpur (Distt. Kangra, HP) mein PNB Bank Building ke paas sthit hai — asaani se dhoondhne layak, aur padhaai ke liye ekdum sahi mahaul. Yahaan Agriculture, Accounts aur Maths jaise subjects ke expert faculty NEET/JEE se lekar HP ke government aur defence exams tak, structured tarike se taiyari karwate hain.

Academy ka focus sirf syllabus complete karne par nahi, balki concept clarity aur discipline dono par hota hai — taaki students sirf exams ke liye nahi, real-life problem-solving ke liye bhi taiyar hon.
